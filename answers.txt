What is the difference between sync and async I/O?         gpt-4o       In synchronous (sync) I/O operations, tasks are performed in a sequential manner, meaning each operation must complete before the next one begins. Async I/O is non-blocking, making it more efficient in handling multiple operations by allowing other processes to run in the background until the I/O operation signals its completion. This can lead to significant performance improvements, especially in applications that need to perform many I/O operations at once.
