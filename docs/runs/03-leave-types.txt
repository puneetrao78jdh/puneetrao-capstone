Sick leave is a type of leave granted to employees when they are ill or need to care for a sick family member. It is typically intended for health-related absences.

Earned leave, on the other hand, refers to leave that employees accumulate over time, often based on hours worked or length of service. It can usually be used for various purposes, including vacation, personal time, or illness.

In summary, sick leave is specifically for health-related reasons, while earned leave is more flexible and can be used for various personal needs.
