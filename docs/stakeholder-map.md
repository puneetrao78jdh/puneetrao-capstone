# Stakeholder Map

## Purpose

This document identifies the primary stakeholders of the **Matellio Employee Policy & Support Enterprise Knowledge Assistant (EKA)**, the challenges they currently face, and the expected value delivered by the solution.

The EKA is designed to provide accurate, policy-based answers with citations from three policy domains:

- Leave & HR Policies
- Workplace Policies
- IT Security Policies

---

# Stakeholder Summary

| Stakeholder | Current Challenges | How the EKA Helps | Primary Policy Areas |
|-------------|-------------------|-------------------|----------------------|
| Employee | Frequently searches multiple HR, Workplace, and IT Security policy documents to understand company rules, benefits, and responsibilities. | Provides accurate policy-based answers with citations through a natural language question-and-answer interface. | Leave & HR, Workplace, IT Security |
| Professional | Needs guidance on policies specific to Professionals, including leave entitlements, workplace rules, and employment conditions. | Identifies the employee category and retrieves answers from the correct policy documents. | Leave & HR, Workplace |
| Contractor | Needs contractor-specific information about leave eligibility, workplace responsibilities, and applicable company policies. | Retrieves answers only from policies applicable to Contractors and clearly distinguishes them from employee policies. | Leave & HR, Workplace |
| Manager | Frequently answers questions related to leave, probation, hybrid working, remote working, separation, and policy interpretation for team members. | Quickly retrieves policy-backed answers with citations, reducing manual policy interpretation and improving consistency. | Leave & HR, Workplace, IT Security |
| HR Support Staff | Repeatedly answers employee questions related to leave, probation, maternity, paternity, separation, confirmation, workplace policies, and employee lifecycle. | Automates responses to frequently asked HR and workplace policy questions while providing citations to the relevant policy documents. | Leave & HR, Workplace |
| IT Support Staff | Handles repeated questions related to password management, OneDrive, SharePoint, data security, backup, company devices, and remote working. | Provides immediate policy-backed answers with references to the relevant IT Security policy documents. | IT Security, Workplace |

# Expected Business Value

The Enterprise Knowledge Assistant is expected to provide the following benefits:

- Reduce the effort required to search multiple Leave & HR, Workplace, and IT Security policy documents.
- Improve consistency in the interpretation of organisational policies.
- Provide transparent answers supported by citations to the original policy documents.
- Reduce repetitive HR and IT support queries.
- Improve employee self-service across HR, Workplace, and IT policy domains.
- Improve organisational compliance with workplace and information security policies.
- Enable managers, HR, and IT teams to spend more time on complex employee issues rather than answering repetitive policy questions.
